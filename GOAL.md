# Northwest Tennis — project goal

Deliver a polished, responsive, portable team website with expressive motion and a phone-friendly publishing workflow, targeting free hosting outside ChatGPT Sites.

## Accepted direction
Green, gold and cream collegiate/editorial design; Next Match, boys/girls teams, captains and lineup positions, schedule/results, recaps, fundraiser link, eventual external team store. No automated live scoring. Optional manual individual-match updates later.

## Milestones
- [ ] Working desktop/mobile public preview and clearly labeled sample content.
- [ ] Local editor prototype with validation and export.
- [ ] Review with Colby and replace sample content with authorized team assets.
- [ ] Connect secure single-owner authentication and shared persistent data.
- [ ] Deploy to chosen free hosting; optional custom domain later.

## Definition of done for first preview
Responsive navigation, team filters, schedule, profile cards, recap dialog, working local editor, reduced-motion support, no invented real player details or misleading donation links.

## Limits
Local editor is a prototype, not authentication or public publishing. Production sign-in, database and hosting require account configuration. Photos are illustrative external references, not Northwest players.
